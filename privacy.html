Privacy Policy

Xylovia AI Uploader respects your privacy.

Data collected

  • TikTok OAuth tokens (access + refresh) to perform uploads you authorize.
  • Video files you explicitly choose to upload.

Data use

Tokens are used solely to call TikTok Video Upload API on your behalf. No data is sold, shared, or used for ads.

Storage

Tokens stored in your own Hermes vault / local env. No server-side database.

Deletion

Revoke access anytime via TikTok Settings > Security > Manage app permissions. Tokens deleted on request.
